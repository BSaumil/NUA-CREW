# NUA CREW — Brand Identity v1.0 (27 October 2026)

## Brand strategy
**Master brand:** NUA. **Product brand:** NUA CREW. **Category:** AI-first jobs and local-shift marketplace for talent and employers. **Promise:** Find worthwhile work faster; hire the right people with clarity and dignity. **Mission:** Make authentic opportunities and verified, fair recruitment accessible. **Personality:** Human, confident, calm, trustworthy, helpful, intelligent; never flashy, desperate or boastful.

## Messaging
**Primary tagline:** Better Jobs. Stronger Teams.  
**Support line:** Real work. Real people. Better matches.  
**Candidates:** Find work that fits your skills, life and ambitions.  
**Employers:** Meet qualified people without unnecessary noise.  
**Shifts:** Fill shifts with the right people, when it matters.  
**AI disclosure:** AI recommendations are explainable suggestions, not promises of employment or automated hiring decisions.

## Visual direction
Use existing NUA palette: Burgundy `#6B1738` as primary action; Deep Wine `#3C0B21` as authority/backdrop; Obsidian `#0B0B0C` for text/dark; Ivory `#F7F3EF` for welcoming background; Gold `#C9A96E` for refined details; Teal `#0FA3B1` for live/status and AI matching. Never use gold or teal as large decorative text on ivory without checking contrast. Typography: Manrope Bold/ExtraBold for display, Inter Regular/Medium for body; system fallbacks. 8px spatial grid, 12px button radius, 20px card radius. Light surfaces and a purposeful, low-noise interface.

## Identity assets
Primary lockup: square icon, NUA wordmark, CREW descriptor, tagline. Dark lockup for hero/footer. App icon: an angular N with overlapping strokes (person-to-person connection), teal connection node, gold supporting stroke. These are new editable vector concepts; do not assert an official trademark registration or that they reproduce any existing official NUA asset. If an authoritative NUA logo SVG is available in the product repository, preserve its official letterforms and replace the conceptual NUA wordmark while retaining the CREW sub-brand system.

## Usage rules
Use `NUA CREW` (all caps) in headers, app display label, marketing. Use `NUA CREW Hire`, `NUA CREW Jobs`, `NUA CREW Shifts`, `NUA CREW Talent`, `NUA CREW AI`. Domain identifiers and package namespaces can use `nua-crew`. Do not use HIRIVO or Shiftmate in current product UI. Maintain clear whitespace equal to at least one-half the icon width around the primary logo. Icon-only is preferred below 48px; do not put the full lockup into a square favicon.

## Experience & accessibility
Job cards display pay as `Disclosed` vs `Estimated`; trust badges require evidence. Avoid manipulative urgency, faux progress, invented match outcomes or predictive interview probability. Support WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard/focus, screen readers, dynamic type, reduced motion, and clear error states. Use accessible color combinations verified in implementation.

## Brand clearance
Working identity only. Verify `NUA CREW` word mark and variants, related marks, ASIC registration, `.com.au/.com/.app` and social handles. Relevant official tool: https://search.ipaustralia.gov.au/trademarks . Do not state availability based on a basic internet search.
