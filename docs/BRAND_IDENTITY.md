# NUA CREW — Brand Identity v1.0 (27 October 2026)

## Brand strategy
**Master brand:** NUA. **Product brand:** NUA CREW. **Category:** AI-first jobs and local-shift marketplace for talent and employers. **Promise:** Find worthwhile work faster; hire the right people with clarity and dignity. **Mission:** Make authentic opportunities and verified, fair recruitment accessible. **Personality:** Human, confident, calm, trustworthy, helpful, intelligent; never flashy, desperate or boastful.

## Messaging
**Primary tagline:** Where Talent Meets Opportunity (approved brand kit, 2026-11-07; supersedes "Better Jobs. Stronger Teams.").  
**Support line:** Real work. Real people. Better matches.  
**Candidates:** Find work that fits your skills, life and ambitions.  
**Employers:** Meet qualified people without unnecessary noise.  
**Shifts:** Fill shifts with the right people, when it matters.  
**AI disclosure:** AI recommendations are explainable suggestions, not promises of employment or automated hiring decisions.

## Visual direction
Use existing NUA palette: Burgundy `#6B1738` as primary action; Deep Wine `#3C0B21` as authority/backdrop; Obsidian `#0B0B0C` for text/dark; Ivory `#F7F3EF` for welcoming background; Gold `#C9A96E` for refined details; Teal `#0FA3B1` for live/status and AI matching. Never use gold or teal as large decorative text on ivory without checking contrast. Typography: Manrope Bold/ExtraBold for display, Inter Regular/Medium for body; system fallbacks. 8px spatial grid, 12px button radius, 20px card radius. Light surfaces and a purposeful, low-noise interface.

## Identity assets
The approved logo is the owner-supplied brand kit (see `docs/brand/NUA_CREW_OFFICIAL_BRAND_GUIDE.md`; files in `brand/kit/`): two connected figures forming a stylised N, the words NUA CREW, and the tagline built in. It replaces the earlier conceptual glyph, which has been removed. Do not redraw, stretch, rotate, outline, recolour or remove words from it.

- **Full logo** (tagline built in): hero, footer, sign-in, splash. Never print the tagline again beside it. On a dark surface set it on warm ivory (`.nua-logo--tile`) instead of recolouring it.
- **Symbol + live text "NUA CREW"**: small navigation bars. The symbol is decorative next to the words.
- **Sizes**: website header uses the compact symbol lockup; the full logo is shown at 180-260px wide where there is room.
- **App icon**: `icons/NUA_CREW_app_icon_ivory_1024.png` (iOS). Android adaptive foreground is the symbol alone on transparent, inside the safe zone, over warm ivory; the notification icon is a white silhouette of the symbol.
- **Vectors** (`logos/*_traced.svg`, `*.pdf`) are automatic traces of the approved raster, not original designer source. Review small-scale text before print. Do not claim a native font-based vector original.
- **Palette** stays as the product tokens below. The artwork's own burgundy (`#890A31`) is used only inside the logo files.

These are working brand assets. Do not assert an official trademark registration (see Brand clearance).

## Usage rules
Use `NUA CREW` (all caps) in headers, app display label, marketing. Use `NUA CREW Hire`, `NUA CREW Jobs`, `NUA CREW Shifts`, `NUA CREW Talent`, `NUA CREW AI`. Domain identifiers and package namespaces can use `nua-crew`. Do not use HIRIVO or Shiftmate in current product UI. Maintain clear whitespace equal to at least one-half the icon width around the primary logo. Icon-only is preferred below 48px; do not put the full logo into a square favicon.

## Experience & accessibility
Job cards display pay as `Disclosed` vs `Estimated`; trust badges require evidence. Avoid manipulative urgency, faux progress, invented match outcomes or predictive interview probability. Support WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard/focus, screen readers, dynamic type, reduced motion, and clear error states. Use accessible color combinations verified in implementation.
