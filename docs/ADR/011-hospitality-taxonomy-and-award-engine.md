# ADR-011 — Hospitality role taxonomy and award-aware pay assessment

Status: accepted (2026-10-29). Builds on ADR-003 (isolation) and ADR-009 (candidate-private data).

## Decisions

**Roles are a search taxonomy, not a legal grade.** `hospitality_roles` holds the 287 curated NUA CREW positions (stable ids `HC-0001`…`HC-0287`, 14 departments, seniority tier 1–8, a separate display order). The workbook's "indicative classification" text is kept verbatim and parsed only into an informational level range and a `needs_review` flag. Nothing in the import assigns an award or a classification. Six titles appear in two departments (for example Restaurant Manager); both rows are kept because they are different jobs, and search always shows the department. Aliases (`hospitality_role_aliases`) cover spelling variants and curated spoken synonyms (waitstaff, glassy, CDP…) and are matched accent- and punctuation-insensitively.

**The employer decides coverage and classification; the engine assesses.** A pay assessment takes: the award (or "enterprise agreement / not sure"), a confirmation that it covers the business and work, a classification, a confirmation that it matches the duties, employment type, worker class and the hourly rate. Without both confirmations the result is `requires_review`, never `compliant_as_assessed`, and a high rate does not change that. Roles flagged `needs_review` (management, trades, other awards) always need review.

**Four honest outcomes.** `compliant_as_assessed` (only for the checks listed in `not_assessed`), `below_minimum`, `requires_review`, `missing_source`. Unresolved instruments, junior/apprentice/trainee, super-inclusive pay and every salary arrangement are `requires_review`: hourly equivalents are never inferred from a salary and adult rates are never substituted. Public holiday rates are computed from an administrator-loaded calendar; with none loaded, shifts are treated as ordinary days and the holiday minimum is shown separately.

**Exact money.** Integer cents and integer basis points; one half-up rounding step to the cent (`apply_bp`). Casual rows hold the full multiplier including the 25% loading (the supplied PDF's own example: casual Sunday 150% × $27.08 = $40.62). Shifts are split at local midnight and timed in real elapsed minutes so daylight-saving days are correct.

**Versioned, auditable rules.** `award_rate_versions` and `penalty_rule_versions` carry `effective_from/to`, source URL/note and `data_status`: `supplied_unverified` (the 29 Oct 2026 workbook/PDF) or `verified` (an administrator, audited, after checking the Fair Work pay guide). The importer is idempotent; re-importing keeps a verification only when the stored figures are unchanged. `pay_assessments` are immutable rows (inputs, result, rate version ids, input hash). Publishing re-assesses against *today's* rules and stores a new row; the earlier row stays as the record of what was true then. Rules for something the supplied data does not state unambiguously (Registered Clubs weekend/holiday multipliers, evening/night, overtime, allowances) are deliberately **not loaded**; the engine reports them as not assessed.

**Server-side gate.** A job saved with a role must carry a pay check to publish (`pay_assessment_required`), the check must still describe the advertised hourly minimum and employment type (`pay_assessment_stale`), `below_minimum` is a hard 422 (`below_award_minimum`), and `requires_review` needs an acknowledgement recorded against the same input (`pay_review_required`; acknowledging a below-minimum result is refused). Shifts re-check their own day's rate. Editing a role-based job with a client that omits `role_code` keeps the role and check; only an explicit value changes them. **Limit:** jobs saved with no role (the quick form, imported listings, existing ads) are not award-checked, because there is no basis to know the award. Making a role mandatory for hospitality ads needs a reconciliation of existing ads first and is tracked as a follow-up.

**Isolation.** Reference tables are world-readable (public taxonomy) and writable only in system/admin scope; the runtime role's writes are filtered by RLS. `pay_assessments`/acknowledgements are visible only to the issuing business and have no tenant UPDATE/DELETE policy. `candidate_roles` is candidate-private (employers never read it; ADR-009 pattern). A pay assessment id from another business is rejected at job save. Résumé role suggestions treat the text as untrusted data: it is only matched against known role names, scanned up to a fixed size, and proposals are saved only when the person accepts them.

## Consequences / not done
Allowances, evening/night, overtime, minimum engagement, leave loading, junior/apprentice/trainee schedules, enterprise agreements and a public holiday calendar are not implemented (listed to every employer in each result). `role_relationship` (career paths) and `role_qualification`/certificates are not built; qualification requirements are jurisdiction- and duty-dependent and were not in the supplied data. All figures are unverified until an administrator verifies them against Fair Work. No hosted deployment of the API, so none of this is live.
