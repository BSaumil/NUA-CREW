# NUA CREW — Official Brand Kit

**Approved tagline:** Where Talent Meets Opportunity

## Approved identity
- Name: **NUA CREW** (uppercase).
- Identity: two connected figures forming a stylized N (talent meets employers).
- Master artwork: `original/approved_logo_original.png` (historical approved image).
- For normal use choose `logos/NUA_CREW_primary_transparent.png` or `logos/NUA_CREW_logo_vector_traced.svg`.
- Vector files are **automatic traces** of the approved raster, not original editable designer source. Review small-scale text before production printing.
- Accent colours extracted/standardised from approved artwork, with the wider NUA palette: NUA Burgundy `#6B1738`; artwork Burgundy `#890A31`; Obsidian `#111113`; Warm Ivory `#F6F0E5`; white `#FFFFFF`.
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline, recolour outside approved light/dark variants, or remove words from the official logo.

## Primary usage
- Website header: horizontal transparent logo; recommended rendered width 180–260px.
- Hero/footer: horizontal master on ivory or white; tagline built in.
- Small navigation: use symbol-only + accessible text label `NUA Crew`.
- Android/iOS app icon: `icons/NUA_CREW_app_icon_ivory_1024.png`; export Android adaptive foreground as separate symbol if needed.
- Favicon: `icons/NUA_CREW_favicon.ico`, or `icons/NUA_CREW_icon_32.png`.
- App splash: ivory background and horizontal master (no extra text over logo).
- Open Graph: `social/NUA_CREW_open_graph.png`.
- Social profile/avatar: `social/NUA_CREW_social_profile.png`.
- Print/signage: `logos/NUA_CREW_logo_vector.pdf` or vector trace SVG; verify traced contours in prepress.

## Rollout checklist
1. Replace all website brand logos (marketing, employer login, worker login, footer, SEO metadata, error pages).
2. Update app home-screen icons, splash screens, notifications, push templates and PWA manifests.
3. Update employer and worker dashboards, onboarding, booking/invitations, messaging and loading views.
4. Replace emails (sender header/footer), PDFs, invoices, certificates, social cards and store listing artwork.
5. Use exact tagline `Where Talent Meets Opportunity` in public metadata and marketing; avoid double-rendering under the full master logo.
6. Retain underlying NUA POS legal entity details without adding personal names.
7. Validate accessibility, responsive/mobile cropping, dark mode, contrast and multi-lingual page widths.
8. Smoke-test production routes after deployment and review search/social caches.
