# Australian launch gates — questions for counsel (not legal advice or declarations)

1. **Brand:** clearance of NUA CREW and variants (IP Australia trade marks search, ASIC names, .com.au/.com/.app, social handles). Do not claim availability.
2. **Privacy Act / APPs:** collection notices, resume and profile handling, cross-border processing (future LLM/maps/email vendors), retention, access/correction, deletion, Notifiable Data Breaches process.
3. **Fair Work / anti-discrimination:** job-ad content rules, candidate filters, human-in-the-loop decisions, fairness testing, appeal route.
4. **Award-aware pay guidance:** accuracy obligations and disclaimers before any pay guidance is shown.
5. **Labour hire / worker supply:** whether NUA CREW Local would trigger licensing or employment-relationship obligations in Victoria and elsewhere.
6. **Australian Consumer Law:** claims such as "verified", match percentages, salary transparency; subscription terms.
7. **Spam Act:** consent for job alerts and marketing.
8. **Source licensing:** each job feed's licence, permitted fields, attribution, retention, takedown.
9. **Identity verification vendors:** contracts, data flows.
10. **Payments:** Stripe live approval, GST treatment.
