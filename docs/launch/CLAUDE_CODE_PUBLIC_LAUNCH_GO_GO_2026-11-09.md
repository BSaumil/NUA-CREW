## Mission / scope of delegated approval
The owner has asked to **launch now**: public registration for (1) candidates/employees and (2) employers, (3) verified businesses posting jobs, and (4) candidates finding and applying for them. Move quickly in tested vertical slices, **but never advertise a live working service if the backend is not ready**.

The owner authorises development, safe independent PR merges, Vercel deployments, and **the final three public launch flag changes once every documented launch gate is green and the legal/pricing decisions are confirmed**. Do not infer authorisation to incur new paid marketplace charges, expose secrets, activate Stripe/live billing, publish unverified jobs, ship native app stores, enable upload of unscanned resumes/RSA PDFs, use AWS/Railway, or transact in NZ. Obtain any missing owner-only acceptance in the actual provider dashboard rather than requesting passwords in chat. Do not wait for arbitrary feature polish once safety gates pass; launch the web hiring MVP with honest limitations.

**Commercial launch policy to implement, subject to owner approval of published offer copy:** candidates FREE. Employers may register FREE. Stripe remains disabled. To enable real public job publishing without payment setup, introduce a transparent, auditable **introductory $0 job-ad entitlement** for verified Australian employers (14-day ad, fair-use abuse controls, no card required, no auto-renewal or surprise future charge). Clearly label A$49/14 days and optional A$30/5-day promotion as **future planned paid pricing, not currently chargeable**. Promotion/reserved top-five is OFF until reviewed payments and capacity limits are approved. If owner does not approve the free introductory publication terms, leave publication closed while making employer registration and draft creation public; never silently create an IOU or misrepresent billing.
